Paper Simulating 500 million years of evolution with a language model

Lesson overview · Hayes et al. / ESM3

How a model learned relationships between protein sequence, shape, and function—and how laboratory fluorescence put its proposal to the test.

AI’s role: ESM3 generated candidate proteins; researchers defined the goal, selected and made candidates, and measured fluorescence. MathematicsNumber theory06 Oct 2026Released

Can primes be built by adding two fixed sets?

Paper The additive indecomposability of the primes

Read the brief · OpenAI

Take every possible sum from two fixed sets of nonnegative integers. Could the answers match the primes, apart from finitely many exceptions? The released result says no, if each set has at least two elements.

This is Ostmann’s inverse Goldbach conjecture. It concerns decomposing the set of primes; it is different from the familiar question about writing even numbers as sums of two primes.

AI’s role: produced by an internal OpenAI model. The release includes a proof and an author-supplied Lean formalization covering the stated result. The manuscript is dated September 24, 2026.

MathematicsNumber theory10 Aug 2026Released

More than two thirds of the zeros on one line

Paper More than two thirds of the zeros of the Riemann zeta function are simple and on the critical line

Read the brief · Claude / Anthropic

Riemann predicts that every nontrivial zeta zero sits on a particular vertical line. This work raises a proved lower bound: asymptotically, at least about 67.25% are both on that line and simple, meaning they do not repeat.

This is an asymptotic lower bound on the proportion as we count zeros higher and higher. It is not a promise about every small batch. And it does not show that the remaining zeros are off the line.

AI’s role: an unreleased Claude system developed the argument. Anthropic reports a separate re-derivation by its mathematicians and has released a Lean formalization. The full Riemann hypothesis remains unresolved.

Counting compatible choices in a network

Paper Lower bounds for multivariate independence polynomials and their generalisations

Read the brief · Lee & Seo / Gemini Deep Think

Choose some points in a network, but never two that share a connection. How many choices are possible? This paper bounds a weighted version of that count, allowing a different weight at each point.

The result extends an earlier equal-weight bound. These weighted counts also describe particle models in statistical physics. This is not an algorithm for finding the largest allowed group.

AI’s role: Joonkyung Lee and Jaehyeon Seo credit a custom Gemini Deep Think agent with key technical steps. The August 5 revision adds a Lean formalization of Theorem 1.4, a two-color extension; that does not formally verify the entire paper.

One fewer multiplication

Paper AlphaEvolve: A coding agent for scientific and algorithmic discovery

Read the brief · AlphaEvolve / Google DeepMind

Multiplying two grids of numbers can take fewer operations than the obvious method suggests. AlphaEvolve found a way to multiply two 4 × 4 complex matrices using 48 scalar multiplications, improving a 49-multiplication construction.

The count concerns complex scalar multiplications. It is not a claim of 48 real multiplications, a proof that 48 is optimal, or a guarantee of a faster program in every setting.

AI’s role: Gemini-powered AlphaEvolve evolved a search procedure that found the exact decomposition. The researchers released results and accompanying code.

MathematicsGeometry14 May 2025Released

Fitting one more sphere around a sphere

Paper AlphaEvolve: A coding agent for scientific and algorithmic discovery

Read the brief · AlphaEvolve / Google DeepMind

How many equal spheres can touch one central sphere without overlapping? In eleven dimensions, AlphaEvolve found a configuration with 593 neighbors, improving an earlier construction with 592.

You do not need to picture eleven dimensions to understand the distinction: finding an arrangement proves that at least that many fit. It does not prove that more are impossible.

AI’s role: AlphaEvolve searched through programs to find the configuration. The release supplies explicit coordinates and a mathematical certificate. This is a dated 2025 improvement, not a claim about today’s record.

For readers who want to get inside an idea.

You do not need to follow a field professionally to reason through a new result. These lessons introduce the tools as they become useful, then use examples and interactive figures to explain the problem, the mechanism, and the limits of the claim.

The explanations are AI-written and anchored to primary sources. Readers can suggest a correction or a clearer explanation, with a passage and supporting source. Suggestions are reviewed before publication; AI helps draft revisions while the research claim stays traceable to its evidence.
