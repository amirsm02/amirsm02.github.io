AI-written, with reader scrutiny

        
A small correction can matter.

        
A useful contribution might be “this bound requires an extra assumption,” “this picture suggests the wrong mechanism,” or “this concrete example would make the definition click.”

        
How review works today

        
Suggestions arrive as issues or email and need manual review. This site does not yet run an automatic AI review or publishing service.

        
The revision process we’re building toward

        
  1. You identify the issue.Point to a passage, suggest a change, and supply evidence where possible.
  2. AI checks and drafts.Compare the suggestion with the cited source, draft a focused revision, and check the affected example or interaction.
  3. An editor approves the change.Review the specific claim and supporting evidence. If the evidence is unresolved, keep that uncertainty visible.
  4. The revision stays traceable.Publish the change with a dated note, a link to the diff, and optional contributor credit.

        
The aim is to make expert feedback useful in small pieces, without asking every expert to author a lesson. AI authorship does not replace checking the claims.
