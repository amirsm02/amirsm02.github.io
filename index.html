Amir Shapour Mohammadi

     

Hi! I'm a 3rd-year PhD student researching theoretical physics at UC Berkeley with Mike Zaletel. I'm broadly interested in condensed matter physics and quantum information science, especially exotic phases in two dimensions. I'm particularly interested in quantum dots and fractional quantum Hall physics, and in using tensor network methods to study them.

Please feel free to reach me at amirsm@berkeley.edu, and view my resume.
