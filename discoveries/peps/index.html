When a quantum state has a compact description

The paper

Polynomial PEPS approximation of gapped square-grid ground states ↗

OpenAI

The preprint claims that a specified class of lowest-energy quantum states on a square grid admits a polynomial-size tensor-network approximation of the entire state.

01

What changed?

Starting point

An entanglement bound across each cut does not by itself supply one compatible approximation of the whole grid.

The reported result

The claimed network has bond dimension at most C L^c and whole-vector error at most 1/L.

The conditions

An open L × L grid; fixed local dimension, bounded nearest-neighbor interactions, a unique ground state, and a gap bounded below independently of L.

The limit of the claim

It does not guarantee an efficient way to find the tensors or compute with them.
