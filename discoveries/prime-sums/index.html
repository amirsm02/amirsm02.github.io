Mathematics / Number theory

Can two fixed sets add up to the primes?

The paper

The additive indecomposability of the primes ↗

OpenAI

The released theorem rules out two sets of nonnegative integers whose pairwise sums equal the primes apart from finitely many exceptions, when each set has at least two elements.

01

What changed?

Before

Earlier work constrained what a hypothetical decomposition would have to look like.

The reported result

The manuscript reports that every such decomposition is impossible, even after finitely many changes.

The conditions

Both sets contain at least two nonnegative integers; every pairwise sum counts.

The limit of the claim

This concerns the set of primes as a sumset. It does not settle Goldbach’s question about even numbers.
