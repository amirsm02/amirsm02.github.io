A stronger bound on compatible choices

The paper

Lower bounds for multivariate independence polynomials and their generalisations ↗

Joonkyung Lee & Jaehyeon Seo

A lower bound on weighted counts of non-adjacent vertex selections now allows a different nonnegative weight at every vertex.

Partial Lean formalizationPreprint 2 Feb 2026Sources checked 22 Oct 2026

01

What changed?

Before

The cited bound used a common weight for all vertices.

The reported result

Each vertex may have its own weight, while the lower bound still depends on the vertex degrees.

The conditions

A finite simple graph; all weights are nonnegative.

The limit of the claim

This bounds a weighted count. It is not an algorithm for finding the largest independent set.
