How to use the tracker

A claim is the beginning
of the record.

Each record has three parts: a sourced account of the result, a before-and-after comparison, and a short route through existing explanations. The paper’s title and authors stay visible.

We distinguish the documented work of the AI and the researchers. Where the division of work is unclear, the record says so. Evidence comes from the linked sources; this tracker does not independently certify proofs or reproduce experiments.

Research timelines include dated releases, revisions, and publications found in those sources. This edition is a snapshot, not a live monitoring service. Saving a record bookmarks it in your browser; it does not subscribe you to notifications.

These records are AI-written. Suggest a sourced correction, a new evidence event, or a better reading step. Updates are reviewed against their sources before publication.
