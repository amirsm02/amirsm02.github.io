A fixed boundary for zeta's zeros

The paper

The Quasi-Riemann Hypothesis: A Zero-Free Half-Plane Re(s) > 7/8 ↗

OpenAI

The released proof excludes zeros of the Riemann zeta function to the right of 7/8, at every height. The full Riemann hypothesis remains open.

01

What changed?

Starting point

Excluding zeros on the line Re(s) = 1 does not exclude a fixed band to its left.

The reported result

A single boundary, 7/8, applies at every height.

The conditions

The zero-free region is strictly Re(s) > 7/8; poles at 1 are excluded.

The limit of the claim

This does not place all nontrivial zeros on 1/2, as the Riemann hypothesis requires.
