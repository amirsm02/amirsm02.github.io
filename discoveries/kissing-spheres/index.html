Fitting one more sphere around a sphere

The paper

AlphaEvolve: A coding agent for scientific and algorithmic discovery ↗

Alexander Novikov et al. / Google DeepMind

AlphaEvolve supplied an 11-dimensional configuration with 593 equal spheres touching a central sphere without overlaps, improving the previous construction of 592.

01

What changed?

Before

A known 11-dimensional arrangement had 592 neighbors.

The reported result

A construction with 593 neighbors.

The conditions

Equal spheres in eleven-dimensional Euclidean space, each touching the central sphere without overlaps.

The limit of the claim

A construction proves at least 593 fit, not that 593 is the maximum. This is the dated 2025 result, not a claim about today’s record.

Source for the comparison ↗
02

What did the AI do?

AlphaEvolve / Gemini

Evolved programs to search for a better configuration.

Researchers

Researchers specified the problem and evaluator, and presented a certificate converting coordinates into a valid arrangement.

What is disclosed: Coordinates and verification code are released; the full discovery system is not in the results repository.
