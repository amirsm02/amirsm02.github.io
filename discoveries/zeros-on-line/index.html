More than two thirds of zeta’s zeros on the line

The paper

More than two thirds of the zeta zeros are simple and on the critical line ↗

Levent Alpöge & Ralph Furman; proof discovered by Claude

The authors report an asymptotic lower bound of about 67.25% for nontrivial zeta zeros that are both simple and on the critical line.

01

What changed?

Before

The paper cites 5/12, about 41.67%, as the previous unconditional bound for simple zeros on the line.

The reported result

About 67.25% in the asymptotic limit.

The conditions

Count nontrivial zeros with multiplicity as the height range grows.

The limit of the claim

The remaining zeros are not proved off the line; the result does not prove the Riemann hypothesis.

Source for the comparison ↗
