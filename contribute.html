A useful update might be “the journal version is now published,” “this claim needs an extra assumption,” or “this introduction explains the prerequisite more clearly.”

        
How changes are reviewed

        
Suggestions arrive as issues or email and are reviewed manually. AI can help compare sources and draft revisions; an editor decides what to publish. Submitting a suggestion does not change the site automatically.

        
  1. Identify the claim or update.Give the relevant page, a proposed change, and a source for any factual claim.
  2. Check what the source establishes.Keep the authors’ claims, released evidence, and independent follow-up distinct. Leave unresolved questions visible.
  3. Publish an approved revision.Keep a dated note for substantive changes, link the supporting source, and add contributor credit if requested.

        
Checking a summary against its sources is not independent validation of the research. Each record should make that distinction clear.
